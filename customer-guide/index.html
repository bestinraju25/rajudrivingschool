Before you visit.

Requirements can differ depending on the licence category, age, application type and current Motor Vehicles Department rules. The lists below are a practical starting point for our customers.

01 NEW DRIVING LICENCE

Starting a new licence?

For our office-assisted process, keep the following information and documents ready.

  • Identity / address proof
  • PAN card, where required for the application
  • Blood group details, if you want them recorded on the licence
  • Medical / eye certificate where applicable
A valid learner’s licence and other application requirements may also apply. We can check your case and guide you on what is needed before submission.
02 LICENCE RENEWAL

Renewing your licence?

For our office-assisted renewal service, please bring:

  • Original driving licence
  • 3 recent passport-size photographs
  • Eye test / medical requirement, as applicable
Renewal requirements can vary by age, licence type and the current rules. We will help you confirm the exact requirement for your case.
03 MEDICAL & EYE TEST

Need the certificate or eye test?

Medical and eye-related requirements can be completed with the support available at our office on scheduled doctor days.
