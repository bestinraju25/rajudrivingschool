Car driving learning path

A useful car-driving lesson should build skills in an order that makes sense. Start with cockpit familiarity and the basic controls, then work through moving off and stopping, gear changing, road positioning and everyday traffic situations. As confidence grows, practise junctions, reversing, parking and other manoeuvres before bringing the skills together on the road.

The Learning Hub and video lessons give you a way to revisit theory between practical sessions. This combination of structured study and guided practice helps keep the learning journey clear, especially for first-time learners in Chalakudy.
