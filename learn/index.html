01 / DRIVING TEST PREPARATION

Know the track.
Then take the test.

01H TEST

H — Car / LMV

Study the H-shaped track and follow the marked A → B → C → D → A flow.

  1. A → BGo straight up the left side from A to B.
  2. B → CUse the centre connection, turn around the marked bend and continue to C.
  3. C → DGo straight up the right side from C to D.
  4. D → AUse the centre connection again and return down to A.
Watch the supplied demonstration
028 TEST

8 — Two-Wheeler

Follow the continuous figure-eight path through both loops, keeping the two-wheeler balanced and controlled.

  1. STARTEnter the lower loop from the marked starting point.
  2. LOOP 1Follow the curve smoothly and stay within the track.
  3. CROSSPass through the centre transition and continue into the upper loop.
  4. LOOP 2Complete the upper loop and continue through the figure-eight exit.
Watch the supplied demonstration
03T TEST

T — Bus / Heavy Vehicle

Follow the T-shaped route A → B → C → D → E shown in the demonstration.

  1. A → BDrive straight up the stem from A to the top point B.
  2. B → CTurn into the horizontal arm and continue to the right end at C.
  3. C → DReturn along the lower side of the arm and move back towards D.
  4. D → ETurn into the lower horizontal path and finish at the right-side point E.

02 / BASIC DRIVING LESSONS

Learn it step by step.

Tap any lesson to open its learning notes. The descriptions keep the terminology and progression of the supplied IDTR guide while turning the classroom material into an easier digital study format.

Understand

  • Doors, seat and steering position
  • Seat-belt and mirror checks
  • A deliberate pre-drive routine
Practice: Start every drive from a safe, correctly adjusted position.

Understand

  • Clutch, brake and accelerator roles
  • Smooth, deliberate pedal operation
  • Coordination before moving off
Practice: Practise each pedal separately, then combine them smoothly.

Understand

  • Steering control and hand position
  • Indicators and other hand controls
  • Gear and handbrake awareness
Practice: Use calm, precise hand movements rather than rushed inputs.

Understand

  • Lighting and visibility controls
  • Wipers / washers and related controls
  • Use controls without losing road awareness
Practice: Know where each control is before you need it on the road.

Understand

  • Correct and timely signalling
  • Signals for turning, slowing and stopping
  • Recognise the difference between intention and action
Practice: Signal early enough to inform others, then complete the manoeuvre safely.

Understand

  • Mirror — Signal — Manoeuvre
  • Position — Speed — Look
  • Observation before changing direction or position
Practice: Make observation a repeatable routine, not an afterthought.

Understand

  • Prepare the vehicle before moving
  • Observe surroundings before moving off
  • Controlled stopping and safe positioning
Practice: Practise moving off without rushing the clutch, accelerator or observation.

Understand

  • Choose a suitable gear for the situation
  • Coordinate clutch release and acceleration
  • Change gear without losing control
Practice: Aim for smoothness and appropriate gear selection rather than speed.

Understand

  • Starting on an uphill or downhill
  • Control while approaching a slope
  • Awareness of vehicle movement and surrounding traffic
Practice: Use the correct control sequence and avoid rolling.

Understand

  • Recognise the need for an emergency stop
  • Brake firmly while maintaining control
  • Secure the vehicle after stopping
Practice: Emergency stopping should be decisive, controlled and followed by a safety check.

Understand

  • Maintain suitable road position
  • Consider lane and road layout
  • Position early for turns and other manoeuvres
Practice: Good positioning makes your next action predictable and safer.

Understand

  • Observe the junction and traffic
  • Apply MSM / PSL
  • Control speed and complete the left turn safely
Practice: Set position and speed before the turn; keep observation active through it.

Understand

  • Observe traffic and road position
  • Apply the correct signal and speed
  • Complete the turn with controlled steering
Practice: Right turns require especially careful observation of approaching traffic.

Understand

  • Approach and stop with a clear view
  • Observe before emerging
  • Turn left with safe positioning
Practice: Do not emerge until the available view and traffic situation are understood.

Understand

  • Position and observation at the junction
  • Judge traffic before emerging
  • Complete the right turn safely
Practice: Take the time needed for a clear, safe gap.

Understand

  • Observe all relevant approaches
  • Use MSM / PSL as appropriate
  • Select a safe path through the junction
Practice: Scan the whole junction rather than focusing on only the vehicle directly ahead.

Understand

  • Observe around the obstruction
  • Check for pedestrians and opposing traffic
  • Move around the obstruction with control
Practice: Create enough space and be prepared to slow or stop if the view is restricted.

Understand

  • Observation and judgement before overtaking
  • Safe position and sufficient visibility
  • Return to the lane only when safe
Practice: Overtaking depends on a clear view, adequate space and correct judgement.

Understand

  • Reverse in a straight line
  • Reverse with left and right changes
  • Continuous observation while reversing
Practice: Keep speed very low and use observation throughout the manoeuvre.

Understand

  • Understand the available road space
  • Use controlled steering and reversing movements
  • Observe before each movement
Practice: Break the manoeuvre into deliberate stages and keep checking the surroundings.

Understand

  • Approach and position correctly
  • Use reference points and observation
  • Finish straight, controlled and safely
Practice: Practise the steering sequence slowly until the entry and final position feel consistent.

Understand

  • Choose the correct approach line
  • Steer into the space smoothly
  • Check final position and surroundings
Practice: Use observation and a controlled steering arc rather than rushing the entry.

Understand

  • Set up the approach
  • Use controlled reversing and steering
  • Check distance and final position
Practice: Use the reference sequence taught by the instructor and keep checking the surroundings.

Understand

  • Observe traffic already on the roundabout
  • Choose the correct approach and lane
  • Signal and exit with control
Practice: Plan your exit before entering and keep observing throughout the roundabout.

Understand

  • Green, amber and red signals
  • Flashing and signal situations shown in the guide
  • Stop / proceed decisions at signals
Practice: Read the signal, observe the road and make the correct controlled response.
