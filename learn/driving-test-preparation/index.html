01 / DRIVING TEST PREPARATION

Know the track.Then take the test.

01H TEST

H — Car / LMV

Study the H-shaped track and follow the marked A → B → C → D → A flow.

A → BDrive up the left side from A to B.
B → CUse the centre connection, turn around the marked bend and continue to C.
C → DGo straight up the right side from C to D.
D → AUse the centre connection again and return down to A.

028 TEST

8 — Two-Wheeler

Follow the continuous figure-eight path through both loops, keeping the two-wheeler balanced and controlled.

STARTEnter the lower loop from the marked starting point.
LOOP 1Follow the curve smoothly and stay within the track.
CROSSPass through the centre transition and continue into the upper loop.
LOOP 2Complete the upper loop and continue through the figure-eight exit.

03T TEST

T — Bus / Heavy Vehicle

Follow the T-shaped route A → B → C → D → E shown in the demonstration.

A → BDrive straight up the stem from A to the top point B.
B → CTurn into the horizontal arm and continue to the right at C.
C → DReturn along the lower side of the arm and move back towards D.
D → ETurn into the lower horizontal path and finish at the right-side point E.
