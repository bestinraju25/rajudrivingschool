Two-wheeler training topics

Two-wheeler learning path

Two-wheeler training starts with the fundamentals that make riding controlled and predictable: balance, familiarisation with the controls, starting and stopping, signalling and awareness of the road around you. From there, practice can progress toward positioning and everyday traffic situations.

The aim is not simply to move a two-wheeler, but to develop habits that support responsible riding. Use the school’s digital learning resources to revisit road rules and signals while practical training builds control and confidence.
