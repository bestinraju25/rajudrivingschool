Prepare in three steps

Learner’s licence learning path

Good licence preparation starts before the test. Build a foundation in road signs, signals, vehicle controls and road rules, then use structured revision to strengthen recall. The practical side of the journey connects this knowledge to cockpit routines, road skills and manoeuvres.

The website’s Learning Hub contains 25 lessons, while the bilingual mock test provides a practical way to check your understanding. The current mock test has 30 questions, requires 18 correct answers to pass, and gives 20 seconds per question.
