Theory topics

What the theory covers

Driving theory is the knowledge layer behind practical training. The site’s 25-lesson syllabus moves from cockpit drill and vehicle controls into signals, MSM/PSL, moving off, gear changing, road positioning and junctions, then into reversing, parking, roundabouts and traffic signals.

Studying these ideas before or between practical lessons can make the road-training experience easier to understand. The Learning Hub and video classes provide additional ways to revisit the same concepts at your own pace.
